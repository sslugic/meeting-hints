Privacy notice

Last updated October 29, 2026

Undertone is built so your meetings stay on your Mac. This notice explains what stays local, the little we receive, and your choices. It's provided by Modini LLC, doing business as Testera Labs.

On your Mac

  • Audio is transcribed on your Mac and never stored or uploaded.
  • Meetings, recaps, tasks, the glossary, briefings and files you add are stored only on your Mac. The full list is in What Undertone stores.
  • Keys and sign-ins for your AI and tools are kept in your macOS Keychain.

Your AI and your tools

When you use your own AI or connect a work tool, text Undertone needs for an answer goes directly from your Mac to that provider, under its own terms. We don't see it.

What we receive

  • AI credits (free or with Pro): requests go through our server to Google Gemini. We keep your email, your credit balance and daily usage totals. We never store what was said or written.
  • Undertone Pro: Paddle, our reseller, handles payment and keeps your billing details. We receive your email and the status of your subscription, to issue your license key. The key holds your email, plan and expiry, and is checked on your Mac.
  • Usage count: if you leave "Count me as a user" on, an anonymous ping with a random ID, the app version, macOS version and language, at most weekly. Switching it off deletes what was sent.
  • Emails you send us, and feedback you choose to give.

This website

No cookies and no tracking. Only if you arrive from one of our ads and allow it, Google Ads measures whether the visit led to a download.

Sharing and retention

We don't sell or share your data, and never use it to train AI. We use service providers to run the service: Cloudflare (hosting), Google (AI credits), Paddle (payments) and Resend (email). We keep account data while your account is active; ask us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of, a correction to, or deletion of the data we hold about you at hello@getundertone.app. To remove everything Undertone keeps on your Mac, quit it and delete ~/Library/Application Support/Undertone.
