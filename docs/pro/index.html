Questions

Does Pro send my meetings anywhere?
No. Pro features run on your Mac like everything else. The license key holds only your email and when it ends, and Undertone checks it on your Mac.
What happens to the AI credits?
Each month of Pro adds AI credits for Undertone's server, which passes requests to Google Gemini and keeps only usage totals. Prefer your own AI? Keep using it; Pro works either way.
How far does a credit go?
About 20 credits cover a one-hour call; Ask and recaps use a little too. Using your own AI instead costs no credits at all.
How do I add Pro to the app?
After checkout we email you a license key. Paste it in Profile & Settings › General. Lost it? The same screen can email it again.
How do seats work in Business?
Each seat is one person on your team. The owner and admins get seats first, then people in the order they joined, and each gets Pro in Undertone on their own. Add seats any time from the Team page in Undertone; you pay the difference for the rest of the period.
Can I cancel?
Any time. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. Payments are handled by Paddle, our reseller, which also handles invoices and sales tax.
