How it works

One loop, running quietly beside every call.

Step 1

Listens in the background

Starts on its own when a call begins in Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack or your browser, even when you're only listening, and transcribes it on your Mac. Audio is never uploaded.

Step 2

Looks things up in your tools

Read-only connections to your Slack channels, Notion workspace and Jira tickets, plus Linear, Confluence, Drive and more.

Step 3

Whispers context in real time

Spots acronyms, code names and questions aimed at you, then shows a one-line explanation, or the facts you'll need when a question is aimed at you.

Home
Today's meetings from your Mac's Calendar, your open tasks, words to learn and how your answers went. A briefing arrives before each meeting.
Tasks
After each call, only your work: what you promised, what you were asked, and questions to follow up. Move them through to do, in progress, in review and done.
Glossary
Every term from every meeting with what it means at your company. Mark the ones you know in one click; More digs through your sources for the people and links behind it.
Spaces
Keep teams and clients apart. New meetings are filed for you, Undertone suggests a space for the rest, and every view filters to the space you pick.
Answer coaching
Your side of the call is transcribed too, so after a call you see what landed and what to tighten next time.
Artifacts
Status reports, slide decks and interactive canvases, written from your meetings and sources with gaps marked [TBD]. Export to Markdown or HTML.
Ask
Chat with one meeting or all of them, with files attached. Answers stream in, and what you tell it is remembered for next time.
Ask mid-call
A chat field under the live hints answers from the call so far, without leaving the meeting.
People
Everyone from your invites with their role and what they work on, your meetings together and what you owe them.
Reminders
“By Friday” becomes a date. A reminder lands at 9:00 the working day before, and overdue tasks turn red.
Glossary guide
Turn a team's glossary into a shareable guide for new joiners, ready to export as HTML or Markdown.
Fix and translate
Right-click a misheard word and Undertone corrects it and listens for it next time. Translate any passage into 19 languages.

Answers in seconds

Undertone answers from what it already knows with a fast model, and only searches your tools when that isn't enough.

  • Answer cards during a call draw on background briefs and your memory
  • Briefs on the topics and people that keep coming up, matched to each question
  • When sources disagree, the newest one wins
  • A stalled reply hands over to the fast model instead of leaving you waiting

Work stays work

Undertone tells work meetings from personal ones. A webinar or a call on your personal calendar is explained from general knowledge, without touching your work tools.

  • Mark each calendar Work or Personal; company accounts start as Work
  • Personal meetings skip your work tools and memory, and their recaps stay out of amem
  • Switch any meeting between Work and Personal from the hints panel
  • Starts on its own for webinars you only listen to, and in more browsers like Comet, Vivaldi and Opera
  • Says who a question was for: you, someone else by name, or the whole room
  • A call that drops and comes back continues the same meeting instead of starting a new one

Speaks your language

Undertone comes in 17 languages. It follows your Mac's language, and you can pick another in Settings.

  • Hints, answers and recaps are written in your language; terms from the meeting stay as they were said
  • Meetings held in any of 19 languages and dialects are transcribed on your Mac

Even better with amem

amem is a memory on your Mac that Undertone shares with Claude Code, Cursor and your other AI tools. Setup recommends it and installs it for you; a built-in memory is there if you'd rather not, and Settings can compare the two on your own data.

  • What you worked on with Claude Code or Cursor is context in your next call
  • Every meeting recap is there in your AI tools afterwards
  • What you tell Undertone is remembered for good

Private by design

No account and no bundled key. Your calls, keys and meetings stay with you; the only thing Undertone itself receives is an anonymous count you can switch off.

Audio
The call and your mic are transcribed on your Mac with Apple's SpeechAnalyzer. Never uploaded.
Meetings
Recaps, transcripts, tasks, the glossary and your artifacts are stored only on your Mac.
Calendar
Read to show what's next. Undertone never creates or changes events.
AI
Your own OpenAI, Gemini or Anthropic key, a local Ollama or LM Studio model, or your Cursor or Claude Code sign-in. Keys stay in your Keychain.
Your tools
Read-only. Undertone only searches and reads; tools that send, post, create or edit are never allowed.
Usage count
If you leave “Count me as a user” on: one anonymous ping when Undertone is first opened and at most one a week, with a random ID, the app version, macOS version and language. Switch it off in setup or Settings.

Don't take our word for it

Undertone is closed source. What's public on GitHub is this site, our security notes, and the format of every file Undertone keeps on your Mac, so you can see exactly what it stores.

To check what leaves your Mac, watch Undertone's traffic with Little Snitch, LuLu or Proxyman during a call. You'll only see connections to the AI you chose, the tools you connected and, if you left it on, the weekly anonymous count. Your audio never leaves your Mac; only text goes to your AI.
